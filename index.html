How a passport fills

Playing is the input.

Finding a game is the part you touch every week. It is not the product, it is how the record gets written.

Matches played 5
01

Find a match near you

Games around you with the kickoff time, the price, the format, how many seats are left, and the weather. No group chat, no asking around to find out whether it is even on.

02

The host picks the squad

Joining files a request, and a request never eats a seat until the host accepts it. When a confirmed player drops out, the waitlist promotes the next one automatically.

03

Arriving writes the line

Turn up, and a geofence check on the server confirms it. That single confirmed arrival is what moves your matches played, your players met, and your reliability. Nothing else can move them.

What the record proves

Presence, not opinions.

Most football apps ask you to rate yourself, then show the number to strangers. Vamo holds the opposite line. The passport carries only what the system watched happen, which is why it means something to someone who has never met you.

Recorded

You were actually there

Near kickoff the app checks your location against the pitch, on the server. Nobody checks in from home.

Recorded

Who you played with

Unique players met, counted once each, across every match your squad actually finished.

Recorded

Teams and tournaments

Squads you belong to, brackets you entered, results as the organiser entered them.

Never recorded

Goals, assists, ratings

No skill score exists anywhere in the system. Proving talent needs evidence we do not have, so we do not claim it.
